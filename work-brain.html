How it works

1 of 5

A new chat does not know your work

Ask a new chat what to focus on today, and it can only guess.

2 of 5

With Work Brain, it reads your folder first

Open the chat on your work-brain folder. It reads your key files, follows the index to what it needs, then answers.

3 of 5

What each file does

Four files hold your work. Your AI reads the first two in every chat and uses the index to find the rest.

The .md at the end of each name just means plain text.

4 of 5

The folder grows as you work

Projects, people and meetings get their own files. Your Work Brain creates them, lists them in the index and moves old ones to the archive folder.

5 of 5

Every chat opened on the folder knows your work

Open a new chat on the work-brain folder any day and it starts with everything your Work Brain knows. A chat opened anywhere else cannot see it.

How to set it up

1 of 6

You need Claude or ChatGPT

A paid plan for either one, with its desktop app installed and signed in.

Already use Claude Code or Codex in a terminal? That works the same way.

2 of 6

Download the folder

Double-click the download to open it. On Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All. Then move the work-brain folder to your Desktop.

If your Desktop is backed up to iCloud or OneDrive, your Work Brain is too.

Download available soon
3 of 6

Open the folder in the app

In Claude, click Code. In ChatGPT, open Codex. Choose the work-brain folder.

If it asks whether you trust this folder, click Trust. It is asking permission to read and change files there.

4 of 6

Say: set up my work brain

Type or say that one sentence. It takes over from there.

5 of 6

It does the rest

When the app asks permission to do something, your Work Brain tells you which button to press.

6 of 6

Opening it each day

Open the app, pick work-brain from your recent folders, and say catch me up.

Using it every day

What to say

catch me up

It tells you where things stand and what is waiting on you.

save this

It writes down what you just did. It usually does this by itself. Say it before you close a chat to be sure.

undo that

It reverses the last change it made.

For everything else, just talk to it.

What it does on its own

ReadsYour files, at the start of every chat.
SavesIt writes down important work without being asked, and keeps a copy of every change so undo always works.
FilesNew projects and people get their own files, listed in the index.
TidiesIt makes small fixes by itself. Anything it is unsure about, it raises the next time you say catch me up.

Adding your documents

Four ways to give it more to read

It offers the first three during setup. All four work at any time afterwards.

Let it lookSay look through my documents, or name the folders. It reads file names first, opens about twenty that look relevant, and shows you what it learned before keeping any of it.
Hand it filesDrag copies into the about-me folder inside your Work Brain, then say add this to my brain.
Add your LinkedIn profileOn your profile, click Resources, then Save to PDF. Drop that file into about-me.
Ask it to checkSay check my documents for the 2027 plan. It looks only where you have let it, and tells you where it found the answer.

Privacy and safety

Where it lives and who can see it

Where it livesIn a folder on your computer. If that folder is in iCloud or OneDrive, they keep a backup. Nothing is uploaded to Nelvo.
Who can see itYou. If you keep it on company storage, your IT admins can see it, as with any file there. What your Work Brain reads is sent to Claude or ChatGPT, the same as anything you type to them.
What it leaves aloneWhen it looks through your documents it does not open files that look private: tax, banking, health, ID, passwords, pay or legal. It never moves or changes your own documents.
UndoEvery change it makes can be reversed. Say undo that.
No guessingIf something is not in your Work Brain, it says so and offers to add it.
It asks firstBefore changing anything you wrote yourself, it shows you the change and waits.

It does not seem to know me

The chat was almost certainly opened somewhere else. Open the work-brain folder in the Code tab (Claude) or in Codex (ChatGPT), then say catch me up. An ordinary chat cannot see the folder.

Using it on a second computer

If the folder is in a drive that syncs, all your files are there on the second computer. Open the folder the same way.

Full undo history stays on the computer you set up on. On the second one your Work Brain keeps a copy of anything before it changes it, so undo that still works.

The other files in the folder

Two files called AGENTS and CLAUDE are your AI’s instructions, and a few hidden folders hold its skills and its undo history. Leave all of these as they are. The file called Work Brain guide opens this page.

Changing how it tidies

By default it makes small fixes by itself and asks about the rest. Say tidy up but only report to have it list what it would do and change nothing.

Removing it

Delete the work-brain folder. Say remove my work brain first if you want it to tell you whether anything else is stored on this computer.
